# ITM collections voice

How every ITM payment reminder should sound. ITM is an IT and AV equipment supplier in Karachi. Our customers are companies, schools and hospitals.

The reader should feel **nudged by a professional supplier**: not scolded, and not treated like a friend.

## 1. Our voice in three words

**Professional. Respectful. Direct.**

- **Professional:** formal and businesslike. Open with the person's title and surname, for example "Dear Mr. Khan". Never use first names like "Hi Ahmed".
- **Respectful:** polite in every reminder, even the firm ones.
- **Direct:** say clearly what is outstanding and what we are asking for. Keep it short.

### Customers who are often late

For customers who pay late again and again, the reminder gets **firmer, but stays polite**. Be clearer and more direct about the outstanding balance and the request to pay. Do not add any threats (see part 4).

Every reminder, gentle or firm, offers help: if the customer has any query, they can call or email us.

## 2. Words we use

- **"Kindly"**, for example "Kindly arrange payment of the outstanding balance."
- **"Outstanding balance"** is how we name the money owed.
- "Should you have any query, please call or email us."

## 3. Words we avoid

- "As per my last email"
- "Urgent"
- "Final warning"

## 4. Things a reminder must never say

- **No legal threats.** Never mention lawyers, courts, legal action or debt collectors.
- **No late fees.** ITM does not charge them, so never mention them.
- **No stopping service.** Never threaten to stop service, support, deliveries or warranty.
- **No "already paid? ignore this" line.** Do not add "If you have already made this payment, kindly ignore this email."
- **No thank-you for their business.** Do not add "Thank you for your business" or similar lines.
- Never blame the reader or suggest they are dishonest.
- Never change an amount. Copy every PKR amount exactly from the sheet (see the rules in `CLAUDE.md`).

## 5. How we sign off

```
Should you have any query, please call or email us.

Best Regards,
Accounts Team, ITM
Email: accounts@itm.example
Phone: +92 21 0000 0000
```

The email and phone number above are practice placeholders. Real contact details stay out of this repo.
