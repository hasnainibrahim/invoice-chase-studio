# Angle B: lead with the relationship

The weekly statement opens with ITM's relationship with the customer, then gives the numbers.

There is no "thank you for your business" line. `brand/voice.md` part 4 bans it and any similar line. The relationship comes first in a different way: it names ITM's role for the customer.

Placeholders: `{customer}` is the company name, `{count}` is the number of open invoices, `{total}` is the outstanding balance in PKR copied from the sheet, and `{monday}` is the date of the sheet.

## Subject lines

1. Your ITM account, {customer}: PKR {total}
2. ITM for {customer}: {count} open invoices, PKR {total}
3. From your ITM accounts team: {count} invoices, PKR {total}

## Opening sentence

As {customer}'s IT and AV supplier, ITM is sharing your account statement as of {monday}: {count} open invoices with an outstanding balance of PKR {total}.
