# Handoff: weekly statement angles

- **Clerk needs:** invoice numbers to match (PO too), what to pay and by when, full bank details, the invoice copy attached, and how to send remittance.
- **Angle A (`notes/angle-a.md`):** lead with the numbers. Example subject: "3 open invoices with ITM: PKR 1,225,500 outstanding".
- **Angle B (`notes/angle-b.md`):** lead with ITM's role as their IT and AV supplier, then the numbers. No thank-you line, because `brand/voice.md` bans it.
- **Pick for a late payer: Angle A.** The voice guide says firmer but polite, and clearer about the balance. A puts the count and total first, so nothing softens the ask.
- **Watch:** B writes "{count} open invoices" with no singular form. A uses `{{invoices_word}}` to get "invoice" or "invoices" right.
- **Template rule:** for a customer whose oldest invoice is 30 days or less, keep the "by its due date" wording from `.claude/rules/reminders.md`.
